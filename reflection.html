Una ang Ginoo

Personal Reflection

Putting God first in my life and in my digital world

“Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.” — Matthew 5:16 (KJV)

Read My Reflection My Portfolio

My Reflection

“Una ang Ginoo” means putting God first in everything I do and trusting Him to guide me in making good decisions. Whenever I feel stressed or face challenges in school and life, my faith reminds me to stay patient, honest, and hopeful. I also try to be honest in my schoolwork and responsible online because I believe that pleasing God starts with doing what is right, even when no one is watching.

There are times in school when I feel tempted to copy answers just to finish my work, but my faith in God reminds me to be honest and do what is right. I choose to do my best because I believe that being honest is more important than getting a perfect score. Even online, I try to respect others and think before I post or comment because I want my actions to reflect my faith and values.

What I Have Learned

Faith

I learned that trusting God helps me stay strong and make better decisions, even when life gets difficult.

Integrity

I learned that being honest is more important than getting perfect scores. I should do my own work and take responsibility for my actions.

Digital Citizenship

I learned to think before I post or comment online, respect other people's opinions, and use social media responsibly.

My Commitment

I promise to put God first in everything I do, stay honest, and treat others with kindness and respect. This year, I will be a light to others by helping when I can, encouraging my classmates, and doing what is right even when no one is watching.
