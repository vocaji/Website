How to be a good person online

Do and Don't

✔ Do

  • Write a kind comment
  • Say sorry when you are wrong
  • Give credit to the owner of a work
  • Check if news is true before sharing

✘ Don't

  • Bully or insult people
  • Copy other people's work
  • Share fake news
  • Post private things about others
