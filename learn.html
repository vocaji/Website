What is online kindness?

Online kindness means treating others with respect on the internet. It includes using kind words, supporting others, avoiding hurtful comments, and thinking before posting or replying to something.

Why is honesty important when creating digital content?

Honesty is important because it helps people trust what we share online. We should post truthful information, give credit to the original creators, and avoid spreading fake news or misleading others.

What can I do if I see bullying online?

If I see bullying online, I will avoid joining in, support the person being bullied, and report the harmful content. If necessary, I will ask a trusted adult for help to make the online community safer and more respectful.

Remember

Think before you post, choose kindness over hate, and let your faith guide your actions both online and offline.
